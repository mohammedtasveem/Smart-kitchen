🌿 Smart Kitchen - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account Information:

  • Name (provided during signup)
  • Email address (for account authentication)
  • Phone number (for WhatsApp expiry alerts)

Food Inventory Data:

  • Food items you add (name, quantity, expiry date)
  • Categories and notes you provide

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Account Management: To create and manage your account
  • Expiry Alerts: To send WhatsApp notifications when your food items are approaching expiry
  • Recipe Recommendations: To suggest recipes based on your available ingredients
  • Service Improvement: To improve our app features and user experience

3. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Meta (WhatsApp): Phone number is used solely for sending expiry alerts through WhatsApp Business API
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text
  • Data transmission is encrypted (HTTPS)
  • Regular security updates and monitoring

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and data at any time from the Profile settings.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: View your data in the app
  • Modify: Update your information anytime
  • Delete: Remove your account and all associated data
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from WhatsApp alerts in settings

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
